Overview

A colourful freshwater favourite.

Red Cherry Shrimp are one of the most popular freshwater dwarf shrimp in the aquarium hobby. Their vibrant red colouration, peaceful nature and manageable care needs make them especially well suited to planted aquariums.

They spend much of the day grazing on biofilm, algae and tiny food particles found across plants, wood and other aquarium surfaces.

The best results come from mature, stable aquariums with gentle filtration, natural cover and consistent water quality.

Water Parameters

  • Temperature: 20–26°C
  • pH: 6.5–7.8
  • GH: Moderate
  • KH: Low to moderate
  • Stable conditions are essential

🌿

Aquarium Setup

  • Mature planted aquarium
  • Mosses and natural cover
  • Driftwood or leaf litter
  • Gentle sponge filtration
  • Plenty of grazing surfaces

🍽️

Feeding

Red Cherry Shrimp mainly graze on biofilm and algae. Their diet can be supplemented with quality shrimp food, vegetables and occasional protein-based foods.

🦐

Behaviour

They are peaceful, social and active throughout the day. Most of their time is spent exploring surfaces and grazing.

🥚

Breeding

Mature females carry eggs beneath the abdomen until they hatch as fully formed miniature shrimp. Stable water and good food availability support successful breeding.
