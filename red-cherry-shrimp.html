Overview

The Red Cherry Shrimp is one of the most popular freshwater shrimp in the aquarium hobby. Its vibrant colouration, peaceful nature and ease of care make it an excellent species for planted aquariums.

Water Parameters

Temperature

20–26°C

pH

6.5–7.8

Difficulty

Beginner

Diet

Biofilm, algae, shrimp food and vegetables.

Behaviour

Peaceful and ideal for community shrimp aquariums.
