Overview

A bright yellow freshwater standout.

Yellow Goldenback Shrimp are a selectively bred colour variety of Neocaridina davidi, recognised for their vivid yellow colour and pale stripe running along the back.

Their colour contrasts especially well with dark substrate, green plants and natural hardscape, making them a striking choice for planted aquariums.

Like other Neocaridina shrimp, they thrive best in mature, stable aquariums with gentle filtration and plenty of grazing surfaces.

Care

What they need.

Water Parameters

  • Temperature: 20–26°C
  • pH: 6.5–7.8
  • Moderate GH
  • Low to moderate KH
  • Ammonia and nitrite: 0 ppm

Aquarium Setup

  • Mature planted aquarium
  • Gentle sponge filtration
  • Mosses and hiding places
  • Driftwood or leaf litter
  • Plenty of grazing surfaces

Feeding

Yellow Goldenback Shrimp graze on biofilm and algae. Their diet can be supplemented with quality shrimp food, vegetables and occasional protein-based foods.

Behaviour

They are peaceful, social and active throughout the day, spending most of their time grazing and exploring.

Breeding

Mature females carry eggs beneath the abdomen until they hatch as fully formed miniature shrimp.

Colour Line

Keeping them separately from other Neocaridina colour varieties helps preserve the goldenback line.

Frequently asked questions

Common questions.

Are Yellow Goldenback Shrimp suitable for beginners?

Yes. They are generally beginner-friendly when kept in a mature and stable aquarium.

Will the golden stripe stay visible?

Stripe intensity varies between individuals and depends on genetics, age, stress and overall aquarium conditions.

Can they live with other Neocaridina colours?

They can, but mixing colour lines may result in less predictable offspring over time.

Do they need a heater?

That depends on room temperature. The aquarium should stay stable within their suitable temperature range.
