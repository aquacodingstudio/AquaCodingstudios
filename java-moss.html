Overview

A versatile plant for freshwater aquariums.

Java Moss is a hardy freshwater aquarium plant valued for its adaptability, natural appearance and usefulness in shrimp and fish aquariums.

It can be attached to wood, rocks and decorations, where it gradually spreads and creates dense cover.

Its fine structure provides grazing surfaces, shelter for shrimplets and a natural place for biofilm to develop.

Care

What it needs.

Lighting

Java Moss grows under low to medium lighting. Strong light is not required and may encourage algae if nutrients are unbalanced.

Placement

Attach it loosely to driftwood, rock or mesh using thread, fishing line or a suitable aquarium-safe method.

Water Conditions

  • Temperature: 18–28°C
  • pH: 5.0–8.0
  • Stable freshwater conditions
  • Gentle to moderate flow

Maintenance

Trim excess growth to maintain shape and remove trapped debris. Gentle cleaning can help prevent detritus buildup.

Propagation

Cut healthy sections and attach them elsewhere. Even small pieces can establish new growth under suitable conditions.

Shrimp Benefits

Java Moss offers grazing surfaces, hiding places and cover for baby shrimp while supporting biofilm growth.

Frequently asked questions

Common questions.

Does Java Moss need CO₂?

No. Added CO₂ can improve growth, but Java Moss can grow successfully without it.

Can Java Moss be planted in substrate?

It is better attached to hardscape than buried. Burying it may trap debris and cause parts of the moss to deteriorate.

Why is my Java Moss turning brown?

Browning may be caused by debris buildup, poor water quality, sudden environmental changes or dying older growth.

Is Java Moss good for shrimp?

Yes. It provides cover, grazing surfaces and protection for shrimplets.
