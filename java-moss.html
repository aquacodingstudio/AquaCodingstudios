Overview

A timeless aquarium favourite.

Java Moss is one of the most versatile freshwater aquarium plants, providing shelter, grazing surfaces and natural beauty in planted aquariums.

It grows attached to wood, rocks and decorations, making it especially popular in shrimp aquariums.

Its dense growth offers protection for baby shrimp while encouraging biofilm development.
