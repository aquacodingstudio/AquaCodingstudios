Species Overview

A compact cichlid with bold character

Distinctive appearance, active behaviour, and plenty of personality.

Platinum Polar Parrot Cichlids are selectively bred ornamental cichlids recognised for their pale platinum appearance and compact body shape.

They are active and responsive fish and can become very confident once settled into their aquarium.

Individual temperament can vary and territorial behaviour may become more noticeable as fish mature or begin breeding.

A well-structured aquarium with hiding places, open swimming areas, and clearly defined territory is recommended.

Aquarium Setup

Space, shelter, and structure

Strong filtration and stable water quality are important, as with all cichlids.

Rocks, caves, driftwood, and other secure structures can help create separate territories and reduce unnecessary conflict.

Leave open areas for swimming and feeding, while avoiding an overcrowded layout.

Decorations should be secure and stable, especially in tanks containing territorial or breeding fish.

Feeding

Varied omnivorous diet

Feed a good-quality cichlid pellet or granule as the main diet.

Suitable frozen or other prepared aquarium foods can be offered as part of a varied feeding routine. Avoid overfeeding and remove excess food where needed.

Compatibility

Choose tank mates carefully

These fish should not automatically be treated as peaceful community fish.

Very small, timid, or delicate tank mates may not be suitable.

Compatibility depends on aquarium size, layout, temperament, and the individual fish involved.

Territorial behaviour may increase significantly when breeding or guarding an established area.
