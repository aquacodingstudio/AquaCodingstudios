About This Fish

Raised with care

Platinum Polar Parrot Cichlids are striking freshwater cichlids known for their pale platinum colouring and compact body shape.

Our current juveniles are being raised from our own breeding pair and will only be offered once they have reached an appropriate stage for rehoming.

As with all livestock sold by AquaCoding Studios, availability is based on the condition and readiness of the animals rather than rushing them into sale.

Final care guidance, size information, feeding details and compatibility advice will be provided when the juveniles become available.
