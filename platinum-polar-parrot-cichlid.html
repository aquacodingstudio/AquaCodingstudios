Species Overview

A compact cichlid with bold character.

Distinctive appearance, active behaviour and a confident personality.

Appearance & Behaviour

Platinum Polar Parrot Cichlids are selectively bred ornamental cichlids recognised for their pale platinum appearance and compact body shape.

They are active and responsive fish and may become very confident once established in their aquarium.

Territorial Behaviour

Individual temperament varies and territorial behaviour may become more noticeable as fish mature or begin breeding.

A structured aquarium with hiding places, swimming space and clearly defined territories is recommended.

Care

Aquarium requirements.

Stable water quality, suitable space and thoughtful tank planning are important for long-term welfare.

Aquarium Setup

Space, shelter and structure.

Filtration

Strong biological filtration and consistent water quality are important for these active cichlids.

Regular maintenance should be used to keep waste accumulation under control.

Aquascaping

Rocks, caves, driftwood and other secure structures can help create separate territories and provide retreat areas.

Leave open swimming areas and ensure all hardscape is stable and secure.

Feeding

A varied omnivorous diet.

Staple Food

Feed a good-quality cichlid pellet or granule as the main part of the diet.

Variety

Suitable frozen and prepared aquarium foods can be offered as part of a varied feeding routine.

Avoid overfeeding and remove uneaten food where necessary.

Compatibility

Choose tank mates carefully.

Community Considerations

These fish should not automatically be treated as peaceful community fish.

Very small, timid or delicate tank mates may not be suitable.

Individual Temperament

Compatibility depends on aquarium size, layout, temperament and the individual fish involved.

Territorial behaviour can become significantly stronger during breeding.
