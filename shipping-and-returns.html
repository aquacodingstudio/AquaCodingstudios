Shipping & Returns.

This policy explains how delivery, collection, damaged goods, cancellations and returns will be handled once online ordering is enabled.

Last updated: 3 August 2026

1. Current ordering status

AquaCoding Studios is not currently accepting online orders. Products may appear on the website for information and browsing, but checkout, payment and delivery services are not yet active.

This policy will be updated before live ordering begins.

2. Delivery area

Before launch, AquaCoding Studios will clearly state:

  • The areas in Malta that can be served
  • Whether delivery, collection or both are available
  • Any delivery restrictions for livestock or plants
  • Applicable delivery charges
  • Estimated delivery or collection times

3. Delivery times

Estimated delivery times will be shown before an order is placed. Any stated timeframe will be an estimate unless a specific delivery date has been expressly agreed.

Delays may occur because of weather, transport disruption, livestock welfare concerns or circumstances outside reasonable control. Customers will be informed where a meaningful delay is known.

4. Livestock delivery

Live animals require special handling. AquaCoding Studios may restrict livestock orders to approved delivery methods, collection arrangements, suitable weather conditions or agreed delivery windows.

A responsible person may need to be present to receive livestock immediately. Detailed livestock delivery instructions will be provided before checkout is enabled.

5. Plant delivery

Aquarium plants may naturally vary in colour, size, shape and growth form. Minor cosmetic differences do not necessarily mean that a plant is faulty.

Customers should inspect plants promptly on delivery and report serious damage as soon as reasonably possible.

6. Checking an order

Customers should check their order promptly after receipt. Missing items, incorrect items, visible damage or livestock concerns should be reported without unnecessary delay.

Where appropriate, AquaCoding Studios may request clear photographs, packaging details and the order reference so the issue can be assessed.

7. Damaged, faulty or incorrect goods

If a product is damaged, faulty, unsafe, misdescribed or supplied incorrectly, contact AquaCoding Studios at: aquacodingstudio@gmail.com

Customers retain any mandatory rights available under Maltese and European Union consumer law. Depending on the situation, an appropriate remedy may include repair, replacement, price reduction or refund.

8. Change-of-mind returns

Once online sales are enabled, eligible distance purchases may carry a 14-day right of withdrawal under applicable EU consumer law.

This right does not apply in every situation. Legal exceptions may apply to certain goods, including goods that deteriorate or expire rapidly, personalised items and other categories listed by law.

Before launch, AquaCoding Studios will clearly identify which products are eligible for change-of-mind returns and which lawful exceptions apply.

9. Return condition

Where a return is legally available, non-living goods should be returned with reasonable care and, where possible, with their original packaging, accessories and documentation.

Customers may be responsible for reduced value caused by handling beyond what is reasonably necessary to inspect the product.

10. Return costs

For eligible change-of-mind returns, the customer may be responsible for return postage or transport costs if this was clearly explained before purchase.

AquaCoding Studios will cover reasonable return costs where required by law for faulty, damaged or incorrect goods.

11. Refunds

Approved refunds will be issued using the original payment method where reasonably possible.

The timing of a refund may depend on the payment provider and any lawful inspection or return requirements. Final refund procedures will be stated before live checkout is enabled.

12. Failed delivery or missed collection

Before launch, AquaCoding Studios will state what happens when delivery cannot be completed, a customer is unavailable, or an agreed collection is missed.

Additional delivery or holding costs may apply only where they have been clearly communicated and are lawful.

13. Cancelling an order before dispatch

Customers should contact AquaCoding Studios as soon as possible if they wish to cancel an order before dispatch or collection.

A cancellation request cannot be guaranteed once preparation, dispatch or livestock transport arrangements have begun. Mandatory consumer rights remain unaffected.

14. Contact

Questions about shipping, collection, returns or refunds can be sent to: aquacodingstudio@gmail.com

Before launch, replace the pre-launch wording with your actual courier or collection method, delivery area, charges, delivery times, livestock arrival procedure, refund times and exact return instructions.
