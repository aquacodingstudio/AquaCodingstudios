Overview

An intelligent fish with individual personality.

Betta splendens are freshwater labyrinth fish known for their vivid colouration, flowing fins and highly individual behaviour.

Unlike many fish, bettas can breathe atmospheric air using a specialised labyrinth organ. They still require clean, heated and properly maintained water.

A well-designed betta habitat should provide gentle flow, warm stable water, plants, cover, open surface access and comfortable resting places.

Water Parameters

  • 24–28°C
  • pH 6.5–7.8
  • Warm, stable water
  • Ammonia and nitrite at 0 ppm
  • Regular partial water changes

🌿

Aquarium Setup

  • Heated planted aquarium
  • Gentle filtration
  • Easy surface access
  • Broad leaves and resting places
  • Secure lid

🍽️

Feeding

Bettas benefit from a varied carnivorous diet including quality betta pellets and suitable frozen or live foods in controlled portions.

🐠

Behaviour

Bettas are curious and often interact with their surroundings. Temperament varies greatly between individuals, especially around tank mates.

🏡

Housing

A betta should have a dedicated, appropriately sized aquarium rather than a cup or unheated bowl. Comfort and stability come before decoration.
