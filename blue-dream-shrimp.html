Overview

A deep-blue freshwater favourite.

Blue Dream Shrimp are a selectively bred colour variety of Neocaridina davidi, admired for their rich sapphire-blue colouration and peaceful nature.

Their vibrant blue colour contrasts beautifully against green aquatic plants and dark substrates, making them one of the most eye-catching Neocaridina varieties available.

Like other Neocaridina shrimp, they thrive in mature planted aquariums with stable water quality, gentle filtration and plenty of surfaces for grazing.
