Overview

A deep-blue freshwater favourite.

Blue Dream Shrimp are a selectively bred colour variety of Neocaridina davidi, known for their rich blue colour and peaceful behaviour.

They spend much of the day grazing on biofilm, algae and tiny food particles found across plants, wood, rocks and other aquarium surfaces.

Their colour stands out especially well against green plants, dark substrate and natural hardscape. Like other Neocaridina, they do best in mature, stable aquariums.

Care

What they need.

Water Parameters

  • Temperature: 20–26°C
  • pH: 6.5–7.8
  • Moderate GH
  • Low to moderate KH
  • Ammonia and nitrite: 0 ppm

Aquarium Setup

  • Mature planted aquarium
  • Gentle sponge filtration
  • Mosses and hiding places
  • Driftwood or leaf litter
  • Plenty of grazing surfaces

Feeding

Blue Dream Shrimp mainly graze on biofilm and algae. Supplement their diet with quality shrimp food, vegetables and occasional protein-based foods.

Behaviour

They are peaceful, social and active throughout the day. Most of their time is spent grazing and exploring.

Breeding

Mature females carry eggs beneath the abdomen until they hatch as fully formed miniature shrimp.

Colour Line

Blue Dream Shrimp should ideally be kept separately from other Neocaridina colour varieties if you want to preserve the line.

Frequently asked questions

Common questions.

Are Blue Dream Shrimp suitable for beginners?

Yes. They are generally beginner-friendly when kept in a mature, stable aquarium.

Will they stay blue?

Colour intensity varies between individuals and may also be influenced by genetics, diet, stress and aquarium conditions.

Can they live with other shrimp colours?

They can, but mixing Neocaridina colours may produce less predictable offspring and gradually weaken the colour line.

Do they need a heater?

That depends on room temperature. The aquarium should stay stable within their suitable temperature range.
