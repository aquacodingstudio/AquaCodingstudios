Privacy Policy.

This policy explains what personal information AquaCoding Studios may collect, why it is used and the choices available to you.

Last updated: 3 August 2026

1. Who we are

AquaCoding Studios is an aquatic retail and educational business based in Malta.

For privacy-related questions, contact: aquacodingstudio@gmail.com

2. Information we may collect

We may collect information that you choose to provide when you contact us, ask a question or make an enquiry.

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • The contents of your message
  • Any other information you voluntarily provide

Our hosting and security providers may also process limited technical information, such as an IP address, browser type, device information and access logs, to deliver and protect the website.

3. How we use personal information

Personal information may be used to:

  • Respond to questions and enquiries
  • Provide requested information or customer support
  • Manage future orders, payments or delivery enquiries
  • Protect the website against misuse or security threats
  • Meet legal, accounting or regulatory obligations
  • Improve the website and customer experience

4. Legal basis for processing

Depending on the situation, we may process personal information because:

  • You have given consent
  • It is necessary to respond before entering into a contract
  • It is necessary to perform a contract
  • We have a legitimate interest in operating and securing the website
  • We must comply with a legal obligation

5. Cookies and analytics

AquaCoding Studios does not currently use advertising or analytics cookies.

Essential technical cookies may be used where necessary for website security or basic functionality. If non-essential cookies or analytics are introduced later, this policy and any required consent controls will be updated.

6. Sharing your information

We do not sell personal information.

Information may be shared only where reasonably necessary with service providers that help operate the website or business, such as hosting, security, email, payment or delivery providers, or where disclosure is required by law.

7. International transfers

Some service providers may process information outside Malta or outside the European Economic Area. Where this happens, appropriate safeguards should be used as required by applicable data protection law.

8. How long information is kept

Personal information is kept only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including responding to enquiries, managing transactions, resolving disputes and meeting legal obligations.

Information that is no longer required will be deleted or anonymised where reasonably possible.

9. Your data protection rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • Ask for access to your personal information
  • Ask for inaccurate information to be corrected
  • Ask for information to be deleted
  • Ask for processing to be restricted
  • Object to certain processing
  • Receive certain information in a portable format
  • Withdraw consent where processing relies on consent

To exercise a right, email aquacodingstudio@gmail.com .

10. Complaints

You may raise a privacy concern directly with AquaCoding Studios using the contact details above.

You may also lodge a complaint with Malta's data protection authority, the Office of the Information and Data Protection Commissioner.

11. Security

Reasonable technical and organisational measures are used to protect personal information. However, no method of electronic storage or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

12. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the website, services or legal requirements change. The latest version will always be published on this page with an updated date.
