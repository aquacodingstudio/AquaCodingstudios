About AquaCoding Studios

Built by hobbyists, for hobbyists.

AquaCoding Studios was created to make aquatic keeping clearer, more responsible and more enjoyable for people at every stage of the hobby.

Our story

Starting small, with a long-term vision.

AquaCoding Studios began with a simple idea: build an aquatic business where animal welfare, honest information and customer trust come before quick sales.

What started with freshwater shrimp and planted aquariums is growing into a broader aquatic brand covering livestock, plants, practical supplies and educational resources.

The goal is not to focus on one animal or one type of aquarium forever. AquaCoding Studios is being built to support freshwater, marine and other aquatic interests as the business develops.

Our mission

Make aquatic keeping easier to understand.

Healthy Livestock

Animals should be offered with suitable care information, proper housing guidance and long-term welfare in mind.

Useful Products

Equipment and supplies should be practical, reliable and genuinely helpful to the people using them.

Clear Guidance

Advice should be understandable, honest and useful without unnecessary complexity.

Looking ahead

Built to grow without losing its values.

What AquaCoding Studios can offer

  • Aquatic livestock and invertebrates
  • Aquarium plants
  • Equipment and supplies
  • Care guides and educational resources
  • Friendly, practical customer support

The long-term vision

The aim is to build a trusted aquatic retailer in Malta where customers can shop, learn and make informed choices in one place.

As the business grows, the range can expand without changing the core promise: responsible care, useful information and a better experience for both customers and animals.
