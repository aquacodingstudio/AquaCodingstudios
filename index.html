ABOUT US

Passion for aquatic life.

AquaCoding Studios was founded with one goal: to make keeping aquatic life easier, more enjoyable and centred around responsible animal care.

From freshwater shrimp and aquarium plants to fish, supplies and educational resources, our aim is to create a place where hobbyists can shop with confidence.

Whether you're setting up your first aquarium or expanding an established collection, we're here to support your journey with quality products and clear, practical advice.
