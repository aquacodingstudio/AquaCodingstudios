About

Starting small, building responsibly.

AquaCoding Studios began with a planted aquarium, a love for aquatic life and a determination to give animals better care.

The business is still at an early stage. The current website documents planned livestock, future products and care information while the necessary aquariums and systems are established.

The long-term goal is to serve freshwater, marine and other aquatic interests rather than focus on only one type of animal.
