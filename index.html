About

This project started with curiosity.

AquaCoding Studios began with one planted aquarium, plenty of questions and a belief that aquatic animals deserve thoughtful care.

The goal is not to pretend everything is already perfect. This website documents the learning process, the mistakes, the improvements and the ecosystems being built one aquarium at a time.
