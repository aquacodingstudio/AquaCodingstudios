Bloody Mary Shrimp

Bloody Mary Shrimp are a striking red variety of Neocaridina davidi, known for their rich, deep-red coloration and peaceful nature.

They are excellent inhabitants for established planted aquariums and spend much of their time grazing on biofilm, algae and other naturally occurring food.

BEFORE YOU BUY

Important livestock information

Bloody Mary Shrimp should only be introduced to a fully cycled aquarium with stable water parameters. Avoid sudden changes in temperature or water chemistry and ensure that copper-containing treatments are not present in the aquarium.

As live animals, individual coloration, size and markings naturally vary.
