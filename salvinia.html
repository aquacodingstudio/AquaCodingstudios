Overview

A useful floating plant for calm aquariums.

Salvinia is a floating freshwater plant that creates surface cover, softens bright lighting and adds a natural appearance to planted aquariums.

Its hanging roots provide cover for shrimp and young livestock while also collecting fine food particles and supporting biofilm.

Because it grows at the surface, Salvinia can also help absorb excess nutrients from the water when maintained carefully.

Lighting

Salvinia prefers steady lighting but may struggle if its leaves remain constantly wet or are pushed underwater by strong surface movement.

🌿

Growth

Healthy plants spread across the surface through new connected growth. Regular thinning prevents them from blocking too much light below.

🦐

Shrimp Benefits

The roots provide cover and extra grazing surfaces for shrimp, shrimplets and other small aquarium inhabitants.

💧

Water Flow

Gentle surface movement is preferred. Strong splashing can damage the floating leaves and push the plants around the aquarium.

✂️

Propagation

Salvinia reproduces naturally as healthy growth divides and spreads. Excess plants can be removed once the surface becomes crowded.

🛡️

Our Promise

Only healthy, clean excess growth will be considered for future availability.
