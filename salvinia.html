Overview

A floating plant that provides natural cover.

Salvinia is a hardy freshwater aquarium plant valued for its adaptability, natural appearance and usefulness in shrimp and fish aquariums.

It floats freely on the water surface and spreads naturally under suitable conditions.

Its hanging roots provide shelter for fry and shrimp while reducing light and helping create a more natural environment.

Care

What it needs.

Lighting

Salvinia grows under low to medium lighting. Strong light is not required and may encourage algae if nutrients are unbalanced.

Placement

Simply place it on the water surface where it can float freely.

Water Conditions

  • Temperature: 20–30°C
  • pH: 5.0–8.0
  • Stable freshwater conditions
  • Gentle to moderate flow

Maintenance

Remove excess plants regularly to prevent the surface becoming overcrowded.

Propagation

Salvinia reproduces naturally by producing new floating plants.

Shrimp Benefits

Salvinia offers grazing surfaces, hiding places and cover for baby shrimp while supporting biofilm growth.

Frequently asked questions

Common questions.

Does Salvinia need CO₂?

No. Added CO₂ can improve growth, but Salvinia can grow successfully without it.

Can Salvinia be planted in substrate?

Yes. Salvinia is a floating plant and should never be planted in substrate.

Why is my Salvinia turning brown?

Poor lighting, surface agitation or unsuitable water conditions can slow growth or cause decline.

Is Salvinia good for shrimp?

Yes. It offers shade, security and hanging roots for grazing.
